We are an established, locally based fencing and landscaping contractor serving Exmouth and the surrounding areas of East and Mid Devon.

Established in 2002, we help homeowners improve their outdoor spaces with quality fencing installations, garden gates, timber and composite decking, patios, paving and hedge maintenance.

Whether you need an existing garden fence replaced, a new feather edge boundary fence installed, composite decking built or your garden transformed with new paving, we provide a practical, tailored service to suit your property and requirements.
